01 — ABOUT

Curious about how machines learn, and how systems break.

I'm an AI-specialised Computer Science undergraduate at Aditya College of Engineering, Bangalore, with hands-on experience across Machine Learning, Data Engineering, and Cybersecurity gathered through three internships and six independent projects. I like taking a problem apart until the underlying pattern shows up — whether that's a spam filter learning to tell signal from noise, or a network packet capture revealing a vulnerability before someone else finds it.
